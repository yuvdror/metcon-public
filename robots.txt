# The AI crawlers — GPTBot, OAI-SearchBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot, Google-Extended
# and the rest — are covered by the wildcard below and are allowed on purpose: being
# readable by them is how Thruster turns up when somebody asks an assistant for a
# workout timer. Do NOT "tidy" this by adding a named block per bot. A crawler obeys
# the MOST SPECIFIC group that matches it and ignores the wildcard entirely, so a
# `User-agent: GPTBot` block would silently un-disallow /admin for that one crawler.

User-agent: *
Allow: /
Disallow: /auth-callback.html
Disallow: /admin
Disallow: /admin.html

Sitemap: https://thruster.cloud/sitemap.xml
