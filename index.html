A clock you can trust

Metcon reads the clock instead of counting ticks, so a 20-minute AMRAP is 20 minutes even with the screen off and the phone on the floor. Cues play on the alarm channel, so you hear them over your music.

🏅

PRs, compared properly

Log a score and Metcon tells you if you beat your best — matching Rx against Rx and scaled against scaled, so you never get a medal for doing less work.

🧠

A coach, not a random generator

It knows your maxes, so the loads are real numbers. It knows what you did this week, so it won't hand you a third grinder in a row.

What's in it

Everything you'd expect from a WOD app, plus the things that only matter once you are standing at the bar.

Every timer format

For time, AMRAP, EMOM and E2MOM, Tabata, custom intervals, rest. Splits with a tap.

The benchmark library

Fran, Helen, Grace, Isabel, Diane, Cindy, Murph, DT, Kalsu, the CrossFit Total — seeded, so it's useful on day one.

Strength log

Sets, reps, RPE, and an estimated 1RM from any set you log — not just the ones you max out.

Plate calculator

Draws the discs at the right sizes so you can match the picture to the bar. Rounds down, never up, and says when it did.

Programs

Linear progression, 5/3/1 with the deload built in, conjugate. Free, because the maths is deterministic.

Your box

Join with a code. Leaderboards per workout, for the people you actually train with.

Progress that means something

Tonnage, PR count, and the movements you have been quietly avoiding.

Works in the gym

Dark, enormous type, one accent colour. Readable at arm's length, mid-effort.
